title: "Activity"

---

**Activity** lists, newest first, what people and coworkers did across the workspace, one sentence per change:

> Scout moved WEB-5 Research competitor pricing pages from Todo to In progress

Click the issue, project, goal, team, channel or coworker in a sentence to open it. **Load more** goes further back, down to the start of the workspace.

## What it records

| About | Changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Issues** | Created, edited, moved from one status to another, assigned or unassigned, started or stopped by a coworker |
| **Comments** | Posted or edited on an issue |
| **Projects** | Created, updated, archived, restored, a person given or removed access |
| **Goals** | Created, updated, moved to another status, deleted |
| **Teams** | Created, updated, deleted, a member added, removed or given another role |
| **Channels** | Created, updated, archived |
| **Coworkers** | Their reporting line changed in the org chart |

It does not record messages in channels, chats with the assistant, or the runs of your coworkers. A coworker's runs, its steps and its tool calls are in its **Monitoring** and **Logs** tabs.

## Filters

- **Project**: only what happened in one or more projects.
- **Who**: **People** or **Coworkers**.
- **About**: the kinds of things above.

**Clear filters** shows everything again.

## What you see

Activity follows your access. The changes of a project you cannot open, or of a channel you cannot open, do not show.
