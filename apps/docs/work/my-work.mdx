---
title: "My work"
description: "The issues assigned to you, the ones you created, and the ones your coworkers hold for you."
---

**My work** gathers your issues from every project you can open, in three tabs.

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Assigned to me** | Your open issues, in the order a day is worked: **In progress**, **Todo**, **Blocked**, **In review**, then **Backlog**. Inside each group, the most urgent first, then the nearest due date |
| **Created by me** | Every issue you created: the open ones grouped the same way, then the closed ones under **Closed** |
| **My coworkers** | The open issues held by a coworker that you created, or that sit in a project you lead. **Working now** marks the ones a coworker is on at this moment |

Each row carries the issue's reference and its project. Click it to open the issue, or change its status from the row. Moving one to **Blocked** asks for a reason, as everywhere.

Done and cancelled issues leave **Assigned to me** and **My coworkers**. They stay in **Created by me**.

<Tip>
  **My coworkers** is where you follow the work you delegated. Assign an issue to a coworker, in a project you lead or on an issue you created, and it shows up there until the coworker is done.
</Tip>
