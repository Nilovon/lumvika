---
title: "Goals"
description: "What your teams aim for, broken into sub-goals, with progress filled in by the projects that serve them."
---

A goal says what a piece of work is for: "Double the newsletter audience". You break it into sub-goals, link the projects that serve it, and its progress fills in as their issues get done.

## Create a goal

Only an admin of the workspace creates, edits and deletes goals. Everyone sees them.

Click **Goals** in the sidebar, then **New goal**.

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | What you aim for |
| **Description** | Why it matters and how you will know it is reached |
| **Status** | **Planned**, **Active**, **Achieved** or **Cancelled**. You set it: a goal is never marked achieved on its own |
| **Parent goal** | The goal it serves, or none to keep it at the top |
| **Team** | The [team](/work/teams) that owns it, if any |
| **Owner** | The person who answers for it |
| **Target date** | When it should be reached |

**Add sub-goal**, in the menu of a goal, creates one under it. A goal can never sit under itself or under one of its own sub-goals.

## Link projects

**Link project**, in the menu of a goal, attaches one of your projects to it. Its issues then count toward the goal.

- A project counts toward one goal at most. One linked elsewhere says so in the list.
- Linking is a change to the project, so it takes an admin or a manager of that project.
- Unlinking a project leaves the project as it is: its issues stop counting toward the goal.

## Progress

A goal's bar shows the issues done out of all its issues: those of its linked projects, plus those of its sub-goals. Cancelled issues do not count.

Each person sees the progress of the projects they can open. Two people may see a different bar for the same goal if one of its projects is restricted.

[Overview](/work/overview) lists the goals with the status **Active** under **Goals in progress**.

## Filter

The **Status** and **Team** filters narrow the tree. Sub-goals fold under their parent: expand a goal to see them.

## Delete a goal

**Delete** removes the goal. Its sub-goals move up one level, and its projects and issues stay, without this goal. It cannot be undone.
