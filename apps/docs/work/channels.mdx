**Channels** is where your team talks, with its coworkers in the room. A channel gathers people and coworkers around one topic, a direct message is between two, and any message can open a thread.

## Channels

**#general** is there from the start: a workspace gets it the first time someone opens Channels, and a person who is in no channel yet joins it.

**New channel** creates one, with a **Name**, a **Description** and the **Private** switch.

| | Public channel | Private channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who finds it | Members who see all Boxes, and admins, in **Browse channels** | Only its members |
| Who joins | They **Join** it themselves | A member adds them |
| Who adds people and coworkers | Anyone in it | An admin or its creator |

A member limited to named Boxes only sees the channels they were added to.

Any member can create a channel. Its creator and the admins edit it, remove its members and archive it. **Leave channel** takes it out of your list: you can join a public one again, a private one needs a member to add you back.

**Archive channel** stops anyone from posting and takes it out of everyone's list. Its messages are kept. It cannot be undone from the app.

## Direct messages

**New message** writes to a teammate or a coworker. A direct message always keeps its two members.

With a coworker, a direct message is a quick way to ask it something: it answers right in the conversation.

## Threads

**Reply in thread**, on any message, opens its thread on the side. Its replies stay there, and the channel shows how many there are.

## Mention a coworker

Type `@` to mention someone: the list holds **People** and **Coworkers**. Mention a coworker, and it answers in the thread of your message. While it works, the thread says **Scout is answering…**.

Who answers which message:

- **In a channel**, the coworkers you mention, if they are members of the channel.
- **In a thread**, also the coworkers that already spoke or were mentioned in that thread. Reply to their answer and they answer again, without a new mention.
- **In a direct message** with a coworker, that coworker, every time.
- **Only a person's message** makes a coworker answer. A coworker never answers another coworker, so two of them cannot set each other off.

Several coworkers mentioned in one message answer side by side, each in its own reply.

A coworker answers one message of a thread at a time. While it is still answering there, a new message in the same thread does not make it answer again, and nothing says so: wait for its answer, then write.

### Add a coworker to a channel

A coworker answers only in a channel it is a member of. Mention one that is not, and the app offers **Add and ask**: it joins the channel and answers. In a private channel, only its creator or an admin can do that; anyone else is told to ask them.

You can also add coworkers from the channel details, with **Add members**. You can only add, or summon, a coworker whose Box you can reach.

### What the coworker reads

The coworker reads the message it answers, and the conversation before it: the last 20 messages of the channel, or of the thread, up to about 12,000 characters. It does not read further back.

Each coworker keeps one conversation per channel, in its own history, named after the channel (`#launch`) or `Direct message`. What it read and answered there stays in that conversation, which everyone who can open the coworker can read. Deleting a message in the channel does not remove it from what a coworker already read.

A coworker only speaks in a channel when a person asks it there. It cannot post on its own, from a scheduled task for example.

### When a coworker cannot answer

The thread says why, in place of an answer.

| The thread says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **your workspace's machine cannot be reached right now** | Try again in a moment |
| **your workspace's machine needs an update** | The machine runs a version from before Channels. Self-hosted: [update the engine](/self-host/servers#updating) |
| **this coworker is switched off** | Switch it back on in its settings |
| **it has no AI model it can use** | Connect a model in its settings |
| **you do not have access to its Box** | Ask an admin for access to the Box |
| **it is not a member of this channel** | Add it to the channel |
| **took too long to answer and stopped** | It had 15 minutes. If its answer comes later, it still shows up. Otherwise ask again, or ask for less |
| **stopped to ask you something** | It needs an approval or an answer, which it can only take in your own chat with it. Open that chat to answer |
| **was stopped before it answered** | Someone cancelled the run |

## Messages

Edit or delete your own messages from their **More actions** menu. The creator of a channel and the admins can also delete others' messages. A deleted message reads **This message was deleted.**, and the replies of its thread stay.

Messages are Markdown. A channel shows how many unread messages it holds.

## Not available yet

- No attachments in a message. Put the file in the shared folder and link to it.
- No notification when you are mentioned, in the app or by email. The unread count is the signal.
- No link to Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp or email: a channel lives in Lumvika only.
