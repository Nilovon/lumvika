**Teams**, in the sidebar, has two tabs: **Teams**, groups of people and coworkers who work toward the same thing, and **Org chart**, the reporting lines of your coworkers.

<Note>
  These teams live inside a workspace. To invite people into the workspace itself, see [Teams and workspaces](/teams).
</Note>

## Create a team

Only an admin of the workspace creates, edits and deletes teams.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New team">
    Give it a **Name**, a **Description** if you want one, and a **Colour**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add its members">
    Open the team, then **Add member**. The list holds the people of the workspace and the coworkers you can see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose its lead">
    In the menu of a member, **Make lead**. A team has one lead: naming a new one turns the previous one back into a plain member.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The lead can be a person or a coworker. A person who leads a team can add and remove its members and change its lead, without being an admin.

## What a team gives you

- A place to see who works with whom, people and coworkers together.
- A team on a [goal](/work/goals), and a **Team** filter in Goals.
- The coworkers of a team know it: a coworker that can see the other coworkers reads the names of its teams.

A team holds no access of its own. Being on a team does not open a project, a Box or a channel.

A project joins a team from its own details: **Team**, on the project's **Overview** tab, set by an admin or a manager of the project. It then shows in the team's **Projects**.

## Remove a member or delete a team

**Remove from team**, in the menu of a member, takes them off the team. Their work and projects stay as they are.

**Delete team**, from the team's **More actions** menu, removes the team and its member list. Its goals stay, without a team. It cannot be undone.

## The org chart

**Org chart** draws your coworkers as a tree: each one under its manager, with its title, its live state and its number of reports. Coworkers without a manager sit at the top.

To place a coworker, click it, then **Edit title and manager**:

- **Title**: what it does, such as "Head of growth".
- **Reports to**: another coworker, or **No manager (top of the chart)**.

A coworker cannot report to itself, and the chart refuses a loop: you cannot pick a manager that already reports to this coworker. Only someone who manages the coworker's Box can change its place.

The org chart holds coworkers only. People are on teams, not in the chart.

## What the org chart changes

**Who a coworker can hand issues to.** A coworker assigns issues only to itself, to the coworkers of its own Box, and to its manager and direct reports. A reporting line is how a coworker gets to delegate to one in another Box. See [Splitting work](/work/coworkers-on-issues#splitting-work).

**What it knows of its place.** Each coworker's title, manager, direct reports and teams are part of what it reads when it checks the other coworkers of the workspace, with the **See other coworkers** capability, and what the `get_team_status` tool of the [Lumvika API](/api/introduction) returns.

Nothing else. A manager does not see more of its reports, and a coworker calls the same coworkers it did before with **Work with other coworkers**.

A title and a manager are changed in the app only, by someone who manages the coworker's Box.

## What you see

Coworkers in a Box you cannot reach are left out of the teams and the org chart you see, as everywhere else in the app. So are their changes in [Activity](/work/activity).
