**Overview** is the first entry of the sidebar. It reads every project you can open and every coworker you can see, and nothing else: two members of the same workspace can see different numbers.

## What needs you

Four counts across the top, each opening the list behind it.

| Count | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **To review** | Issues in **In review**, in every project you can open that is not archived. Not only yours |
| **Blocked** | Issues in **Blocked**, counted the same way |
| **Waiting in conversations** | Conversations in which a coworker stopped to ask you something: an approval, a question, an app to connect |
| **Assigned to me** | Your open issues: anything assigned to you that is not done or cancelled |

The first three lead to [Review](/work/review), the last one to [My work](/work/my-work).

## Coworkers right now

Each coworker with its live state: **Working**, **Waiting on a person**, **Needs a look** (it is in error), **Idle** or **Off** (switched off, or offline). A coworker holding an issue says which one: `Working on WEB-5: Research competitor pricing pages`.

The list shows the busiest first and folds the rest into a count. Click a coworker to open it on the board. **Open the office** goes to the board itself.

## Active projects

The projects with the status **Active**, each with how many of its issues are done, and how many wait in review or are blocked. The projects with the most waiting on people come first.

## Goals in progress

The goals with the status **Active**, nearest due date first, with the progress of the issues of their projects. See [Goals](/work/goals).

## Recent activity

The last few entries of [Activity](/work/activity). **See all activity** opens the full trail.

## Issue throughput

How many issues were created and how many were done each day, over the last 14 days, in your own time zone. **Show as table** swaps the chart for the numbers.

## A new workspace

A workspace with no project and no coworker shows **Welcome to your workspace** instead, with three steps: **Recruit a coworker**, **Create a project**, **Assign an issue**.
