---
title: "Chat"
description: "A plain assistant for a question, a draft or a bit of research, without setting up a coworker."
---

**Chat** is an assistant every member of the workspace can talk to. It is not a coworker: it has no Box, no skills, no schedule and no memory. Use it to think something through, draft a text or look something up. When the work recurs or has to act in your tools, give it to a coworker instead.

## Start a chat

Click **Chat** in the sidebar, then **New chat**, and write. Four suggestions on the empty screen send a first message for you.

The chat is named after its first message. Rename it from its **Chat options** menu (the `⋯` on the row or in the header). Your chats are listed by when you last wrote in them, and **Search chats** filters them by name.

## Pick a model

The model picker under the message box works like a coworker's: Lumvika models, your Claude or ChatGPT subscription, or your own API keys. See [Model and provider](/agents/configure#model-and-provider).

The model you pick applies to that chat. An admin can also make it the default of Chat for the whole workspace.

## Add a project

**Add a project**, in the chat header, attaches one of the projects you can open. The assistant then reads that project's **Instructions for coworkers** and follows them in its answers.

It reads the instructions only. It does not see the project's issues, comments or progress. If you lose access to the project later, its instructions stop reaching the chat.

## What the assistant can do

It has four tools:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `web_search` | Searches the web. On a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, the subscription's own search takes its place |
| `web_fetch` | Reads a web page |
| `brain_search` | Searches the Brain, the pages about your company your team validated |
| `brain_read` | Reads a page of the Brain |

And nothing else:

- **No files.** You cannot attach a file, and it has no folder of its own.
- **No memory.** It does not remember your other chats, and keeps nothing once a chat is over.
- **No access to your tools.** No apps, no shell, no browser, no database, no way to send an email or touch your accounts.
- **No view of the work.** It cannot read your issues, your channels or what your coworkers do.

It answers in the language you write in.

## Private and shared

A chat is yours. No other member sees it in their list or can open it.

**Share with the workspace**, in the **Chat options** menu, lets the other members read it. They find it under **Shared with me**, read only: only you write in it, rename it or change its project. When the chat has a project, only the members who can open that project see it. **Stop sharing** makes it private again.

<Warning>
  Private means private in the app. The conversation is stored on your workspace's machine, like every coworker conversation, so the people who operate that machine can technically read it: Lumvika for a workspace on Lumvika Cloud, whoever runs the server on a self-hosted install. The AI provider of the model you picked receives your messages to answer them.
</Warning>

## Archive or delete

**Archive** takes the chat out of your list without erasing anything. It also stops a shared chat from showing to the others. **Show archived** lists them again, and **Restore** brings one back.

**Delete** erases the chat for good, for you and for anyone you shared it with. Lumvika removes it from the list, then erases the conversation on your workspace's machine: its messages, the steps and tool calls of each answer, and the pages the assistant read for it. It cannot be undone.

Two cases keep the messages on the machine, and the app says so when it happens:

- The machine runs an older version of Lumvika that cannot erase them.
- The machine did not answer.

Either way the chat is gone from the list. On a self-hosted install, a backup of the engine data-dir taken before the deletion still holds the conversation.

## When Chat cannot answer

| The screen says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat needs a server** | Your workspace has no machine yet. It gets one when you add your first coworker |
| **Update your server to use Chat** | Your workspace's machine runs a version of Lumvika from before Chat. Self-hosted: see [Updating](/self-host/servers#updating) |
| **Your server is not answering** | The machine cannot be reached right now. **Try again** in a moment |
| **Connect an AI provider** | No model is available to answer. Connect a provider or a subscription |
