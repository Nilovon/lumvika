## Before you start

You need an account on [app.rerun.build](https://app.rerun.build). Rerun starts with a 5 day free trial.

Nothing to install. Everything happens in the browser.

## 1. Sign up

Open [app.rerun.build](https://app.rerun.build). You land on the board, the isometric view where your Boxes and coworkers live, with a sign-in dialog on top.

Right after signing up you answer three short questions about your sector, your team size and your role. They only decide which templates you get shown first.

## 2. Pick your first coworker

The template library opens on its own, filtered on the sector you picked. Two ways forward:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Install a template">
    A template is a setup that already works: a prompt, skills, scheduled tasks and the apps it needs. Pick one, and Rerun creates the coworkers it contains in your account.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Start from scratch">
    Click **Start from scratch**, give the coworker a name, and describe what you want it to do in the chat. It writes its own prompt from your description.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 3. Place it on the board

The toolbar switches to placement mode. Tap a tile and the coworker takes its spot.

Your first coworker needs a Box, and Rerun draws one for you. Behind it, your workspace claims its machine from a pool of ready ones: a private cloud computer, isolated from every other account, that hosts every coworker you place. It takes a few seconds.

## 4. Run the guided setup

A coworker that came from a template usually needs to be connected to your own accounts. When it does, the chat opens on a **Start installation** button instead of a message box.

Click it and the coworker walks you through its own setup: it tells you what it needs, offers to install the apps it is missing, and asks you for the values only you can give. You answer in the chat, exactly like a conversation.

<Note>
  When it needs an API key, the coworker opens a masked form. The value goes straight to your machine and is never shown to the coworker or stored in the conversation. Never paste a secret into the chat itself.
</Note>

When the setup is done, the coworker switches on the scheduled tasks and webhooks it shipped with, and tells you what it can do and when it will next run.

## 5. Talk to it

You now have a normal message box. Ask it for something.

While it works you see every step: the files it reads, the searches it runs, the tools it calls. If it needs a decision, it stops and asks you, right in the chat.

## 6. Let it run without you

Two ways to make a coworker work while you are away.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Scheduled tasks" icon="clock" href="/agents/automations#scheduled-tasks">
    A cron and an instruction. Every Monday at 9, summarise last week and email it to me.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Webhook triggers" icon="webhook" href="/agents/automations#webhook-triggers">
    A public URL. When Stripe posts a refund, handle it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can add both from the coworker panel, or just ask the coworker to set them up for you.
